Signal vs true distance

Dots: signal readings with a GPS fix at the same moment. Rings: calibration points. Lines: the current model for each node. If the dots don't follow the lines, calibrate.

Why calibrate

Signal strength turns into distance with two numbers per node: the signal at 1 m and how fast it fades (n). They change with the site, so fit them from your own data. Every change recomputes all loaded flights.

Option 1: tape measure

Before takeoff, leave the drone at a measured distance from a node for about 20 s (try 2, 5, 10 and 20 m). After loading the flight, drag the timeline to that moment and press "Use signal here", or type the signal in.

Option 2: from a GPS flight

Uses every signal reading that came with a GPS fix, from all loaded flights. Fly at a mix of distances from the nodes.

General

Math settings

How the math works

1. Node positions are turned into east/north meters around the first node.

2. Distance from signal: d = 10^((RSSI@1m − RSSI) / (10·n)), using the median of the last N readings.

3. That distance is a slant line up to the drone, so the flight height turns it into a distance along the ground.

4. Two nodes: the two distance circles cross at two mirror-image spots; the drone's side setting picks one and it follows the track from there. Three or more nodes: least squares, no mirror problem.

5. IMU only: the same dead reckoning as ME74_dashboard_2D.html (gravity removed, integrated twice, velocity reset when still, each move's drift taken out when it stops), turned to compass directions with the nose setting.

6. Radio + IMU: a Kalman filter where the IMU acceleration predicts the motion and each radio fix corrects it.

Log format

Each drone saves one reading per line to its memory, starting with its own time in ms:

12034,RSSI,A,D1,-67      signal between node A and drone D1
12040,GPS,D1,42.4075120,-71.1190400,21.4,9,0.9,153012.00
12052,IMU,D1,t_us,ax,ay,az,gx,gy,gz,mx,my,mz,roll,pitch,heading,dt_ms,overruns
12060,ALT,D1,8.2         height above takeoff (m)

The last GPS field is the GPS's UTC time (hhmmss.ss). It lines the two drones' logs up; without it, logs start together and you can shift them by hand. The existing ME74 firmware printout also works with a time in front of each line: [IMU] LOG,..., [GPS] {...}, and [LORA] RX from node 1 followed by [LORA] RSSI: -71 dBm (node 1 = A, node 2 = B). "Read log from drone" sends dump and reads lines until END.
