Test procedure (quick version)
  1. Flash board A with radio_node1 and board B with radio_node2.
  2. Plug both in. Open this page in Chrome, Connect to one board. Optional: a second tab for the other board.
  3. Wait for the green "Link OK" banner. TX OK should climb every 3 s, packets RX every 3 s.
  4. Place the boards exactly 1.0 m apart (same height, antennas parallel). Calibrate at 1.0.
  5. Move to 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 m. At each, type the tape distance and Record point.
  6. Check the fit. Send the fitted n to the board.
  7. Turn on Validation and record new distances (e.g. 3, 5, 7, 9 m). Check their error in the Validation table.
  8. Download CSV before closing the page.
